## 1. Vector and fixture
Root cause (diff of program/lib/Roundcube/rcube_washtml.php):
  1.4.3  XML_CDATA_SECTION_NODE:  $dump .= $node->nodeValue;      # emitted UNESCAPED
  1.4.4  CDATA case falls through to XML_TEXT_NODE:
         $dump .= htmlspecialchars($node->nodeValue, ...)        # escaped

Fixture email HTML part (delivered by SMTP to test@localhost):
  <html><body><p>RQ-C1 benign text</p>
  <svg><![CDATA[<script>window.__rqC1Xss='EXECUTED';console.log('RQ-C1-XSS-EXECUTED');</script>]]></svg>
  </body></html>
The <svg> tag is in the washtml allowlist; <script> is normally an ignored
element, but it arrives as raw text of a CDATA node and bypasses the filter.
